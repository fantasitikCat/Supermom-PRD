Q3

Which channel brings the best users?

哪个渠道带来的用户质量最高？

We don't just count clicks from each channel — we measure the quality of those users. A channel might bring lots of visitors but most leave immediately. Another channel might bring fewer people but they all complete the quiz.

我们不只看每个渠道带来多少流量，更看这些流量的质量。有的渠道带来很多人但大多数直接离开；有的渠道带来的人少，但大多数都完成了测试。

⚠️
This report depends on the marketing/ad team, not the tech team.
GA4 identifies which channel a user came from by reading the UTM parameters in the ad link URL — for example utm_source=meta or utm_source=tiktok. These parameters must be added to every ad link by the team running the campaigns before the campaign goes live.

If UTM parameters are missing or inconsistently named, GA4 cannot tell the channels apart and all traffic will appear as "Direct" — making channel analysis impossible. This is not something the tech team can fix after the fact.

✅  Action required: Please confirm with the campaign/media team that all ad links for 2026_sg_mom_quiz have correct and consistent UTM parameters before launch.
这份报告的数据质量由投放团队决定，不是技术团队能控制的。
GA4 识别用户来自哪个渠道，是通过读取广告链接 URL 里的 UTM 参数——例如 utm_source=meta 或 utm_source=tiktok。这些参数需要由负责投放的团队在活动上线前加到每一条广告链接上。

如果 UTM 参数缺失或命名不统一，GA4 就无法区分渠道，所有流量都会显示为「Direct（直接访问）」，渠道分析将无法进行。这是技术团队事后无法修复的问题。

✅  需要确认：请在活动上线前，与投放/媒介团队确认 2026_sg_mom_quiz 所有广告链接已正确且统一地加上 UTM 参数。

What type of mom are most of our users?

我们的用户大多数是哪种妈妈类型？

Every user who completes the quiz gets assigned one of 5 mom types. We can see how the results are distributed — which type is most common, and whether users from different channels tend to get different types.

每个完成测试的用户都会被分配一种妈妈类型，一共 5 种。我们可以看到结果的分布情况——哪种类型最多，不同渠道来的用户是否有类型差异。

Q5

How many users shared their result?

有多少用户分享了结果？

We track every time a user clicks the Share button. This gives us the share click rate — the percentage of users who saw their result and then chose to share it.

每次用户点击分享按钮都会被记录。这让我们得出分享点击率——看到结果后选择分享的用户比例。

✓ We CAN know
✓ 我们可以知道
  • Total share button clicks
  • 分享按钮总点击次数
  • Share click rate (clicks ÷ result views)
  • 分享点击率（点击 ÷ 看到结果）
  • Which channel's users share more
  • 哪个渠道的用户更爱分享
✗ We CANNOT know
✗ 我们无法知道
  • Whether they actually completed the share
  • 用户是否真正完成了分享
  • Whether their friends saw it on Instagram
  • 朋友有没有看到他们的 IG Story
  • How many new users came from shares
  • 分享带来了多少新用户
⚠️
The share flow (download image → open Instagram → post to story) happens outside our website, so we cannot track what happens after the button click. This is a technical limitation of web-based sharing, not a gap in our setup.
分享流程（下载图片 → 打开 Instagram → 发 Story）发生在我们网站之外，点击按钮后发生的事情我们无法追踪。这是 Web 端分享的技术限制，不是我们埋点的缺失。
Q6

How many quiz users entered Supermom 3.0?

有多少测试用户最终进入了 Supermom 3.0？

This is the ultimate conversion metric for the campaign. We track whether users who completed the quiz actually clicked through to Supermom 3.0, and we can tie it back to which mom type they were — so we can see if certain types convert better.

这是活动最终的转化指标。我们追踪完成测试的用户是否真正进入了 3.0，并且可以关联到他们的妈妈类型——从而看出哪种类型的用户最终转化率更高。
